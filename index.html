THE DAILY THREAT

Can you detect what others miss?

Attackers rarely break in. They ask politely, in a message that looks like every other message in your inbox.

📧
Email phishingFake alerts, invoices and password resets that borrow a real brand's tone and push you toward a login page that isn't theirs.
📱
SmishingShort text messages with a shortened link, a deadline and just enough detail to feel personal.
🌐
Fake websitesPixel-accurate login pages living on a lookalike domain, often with a valid padlock that proves nothing about who owns it.

ABOUT

About PhishGuard

PhishGuard is an educational cybersecurity awareness platform designed to help users recognize phishing, smishing, fake websites, QR-based attacks and other social-engineering techniques.

Built for cybersecurity awareness

This is a college symposium educational project. Every email, message, website and QR scenario in it is invented for training. Brand names, domains, reference numbers and people are fictional, and any resemblance to a real organisation is unintentional.

What this project does not do

  • The participant quiz never requests real account passwords, OTPs or payment details. The admin password is only for the configurable demo login.
  • Login fields in the mockups are visual only — they are disabled and submit nothing.
  • Links inside scenarios are not clickable and lead nowhere.
  • The symposium portal stores participant details and quiz attempts in this browser for the organizer to review.
  • This static demo has no backend; client-side admin login is not production-grade authentication.

How to run it at a stand

Open the page and choose ENTER QUIZ. Participants provide their symposium details, then take three rounds of 10, 10 and 5 questions without seeing answer feedback or scores.
